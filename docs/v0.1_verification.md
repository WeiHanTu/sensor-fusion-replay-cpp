## Retained projection benchmark

- Report: [`results/projection_benchmark_apple_m4_pro.json`](results/projection_benchmark_apple_m4_pro.json)
- SHA-256: `3db70e884f24faa676fa4b4bd0e0790920e2c28e80024e3747a81c14b1988123`
- Schema: `1.2.0`
- Implementation commit: `185e287ca41e1696d0752570b6dba36780639af3`
- Build: clean `Release`, AppleClang 16.0.0.16000026
- Host: Darwin 25.6.0, arm64, Apple M4 Pro
- Workload: 100,000 authored deterministic finite points per sample, 10 warm-up
  iterations, 100 measured iterations
- Latency: min `0.554 ms`, mean `0.584 ms`, p50 `0.568 ms`, p95 `0.648 ms`,
  p99 `0.668 ms`, max `0.679 ms`
- Aggregate throughput: `171.3 million points/s`; rate at p50 latency:
  `176.0 million points/s`
- Accounting per iteration: 70,000 visible + 15,000 behind/near + 15,000
  outside image = 100,000 input; all 10 warm-up and 100 measured iterations
  were checked
- Percentiles: nearest-rank

This measures SE(3) transformation, rectified projection, classification, and
output-vector construction. It excludes fixture generation, validation, image
decode, visualization, serialization, queues, synchronization, and replay
pacing. See [`benchmark_walkthrough.md`](benchmark_walkthrough.md).
